Good to Know

Frequently Asked Questions

How does the dawn and dusk operation work?+

The door opens in the morning and closes after dark using a built-in light sensor. You can also set your own fixed open and close times.

Can I set my own opening and closing times?+

Yes. Set your own fixed times on the controller, or leave it in automatic light-based mode.

What type of feed can I use?+

Standard chicken pellets and mixed grain, up to around 8 mm in size.

How much feed does it hold?+

Up to 20 kg in the hopper — roughly one full feed bag.

How long do the batteries last, and are they included?+

It runs on 4 × AA batteries (included), lasting around 6–8 months of daily use.

Is it waterproof or weather-resistant?+

It's weather-resistant galvanised steel built for permanent outdoor use — not fully submersible.

Does it stop rats and other pests?+

By closing after dark it removes the easy overnight food source, helping discourage pests. It is not claimed to be fully rat- or possum-proof.

What happens if the door meets an obstruction?+

The door has a safety edge and reverses if it meets an obstruction while closing.

Do you ship internationally, and what's the warranty?+

Yes — we ship worldwide, with a 12-month warranty and spare parts available on request.
